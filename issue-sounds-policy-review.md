Instrument Atlas Sounds includes policy-aware source resolution.

Current policy examples include:

- client-safe
- saas-safe
- commercial-safe
- commercial-safe-no-output-attribution
- commercial-safe-with-attribution
- permissive-only
- cc0-only

This issue is for feedback on whether the policy names and resolver output are clear for consuming applications.

Especially useful feedback:

- confusing field names
- missing compliance signals
- policy names that could be misunderstood
- cases where client-safe and SaaS-safe should behave differently
